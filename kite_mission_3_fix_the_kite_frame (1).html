KITE MODULE • MISSION 3

🪁 Fix the Kite Frame!

Your class is preparing a large decorative kite for the school Mathematics Festival. Before it can be displayed, determine the correct measurement of one of its support rods.

📘 The Problem

In kite ABCD, support rods AC and BD form the diagonals and intersect at point E. Diagonal AC bisects diagonal BD.

BE = 3x + 2
DE = 5x − 10
🎯 Your Mission: Find the value of x and the total length of diagonal BD.
◇

A (top)   B — E — D   C (bottom)

BE = 3x + 2    DE = 5x − 10

AC bisects BD at E

🔵 Step 1: Understand the Problem

What information is given about diagonal BD?

🟣 Step 2: Devise a Plan

Which equation should you use to find x?

🟠 Step 3: Carry Out the Plan

3x + 2 = 5x − 10

What is the value of x?

🟠 Step 3: Continue

BE = 3(6) + 2    DE = 5(6) − 10

What is the total length of diagonal BD?

🟢 Step 4: Look Back

How can you check that your answer is correct?

🎉 Mission Complete!

x = 6

BE = 3(6)+2 = 20
DE = 5(6)−10 = 20

BD = 20 + 20 = 40 units

Since BE = DE, the diagonal is correctly bisected. You successfully determined the measurement needed to complete the kite frame!
